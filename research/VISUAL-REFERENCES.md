# Existing-app visual references

These are third-party images linked from the developers' sites or their repositories, separate from the original Pearl concept images. They are inspiration references, not assets approved for inclusion in the app. They may depict an earlier version; no live app session was tested.

## Swipewipe: dates and finite sessions

[Feature page](https://swipewipe.com/features) · [Screenshot](https://swipewipe.com/medias/screens/on-this-day.webp)

![Swipewipe date review](https://swipewipe.com/medias/screens/on-this-day.webp)

Take the clear date entry point and resumable progress. Pearl keeps a quieter palette and folds deeper options into the relevant screen.

[Product page](https://www.cleverfiles.com/clever-cleaner/) · [Screenshot](https://www.cleverfiles.com/clever-cleaner/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/similarsImageCC3.0.png)

## Slidebox: quick album filing

[Product page and animated demo](https://slidebox.co/) · [Developer demo GIF](https://slidebox.co/img/demo.gif)

The useful interaction is one-tap album assignment while reviewing a large photo. Its animation is linked rather than packaged into the project.
